Study guide for public health majors

Master public health, one term at a time.

Learn the vocabulary of epidemiology, biostatistics, environmental health, and global health. Review with flashcards, lock it in by typing each definition from memory, then test yourself with practice questions.

The curriculum

Choose a discipline

Each discipline is broken into units of eight core terms, with flashcards and practice questions for every unit.

The method

Three ways to study every term

01 — RECOGNIZE

Flashcards

Flip through a unit, star the tricky ones, and sort every card into “know it” or “still learning.” Review just the cards you’re missing.

02 — RECALL

Type the definition

Retyping a definition from the screen forces precision. Accuracy and time are tracked, and a term is mastered once you type it error-free.

03 — APPLY

Practice questions

Multiple-choice questions at the bottom of each unit check that you can use a concept, not just recite it.
